RYS – Record Your Screen: Yordam

Yozishni boshlash

  1. "Yozib olish" bo'limida ekran yoki oynani tanlang.
  2. Kerak bo'lsa mikrofon va kamerani yoqing (kamera pufakchasini sichqoncha bilan istalgan joyga suring).
  3. Yozishni boshlash tugmasini bosing yoki ⌃⌥R ni bosing.
  4. To'xtatish: ⌃⌥R yoki menyu panelidagi ● → "Yozishni to'xtatish".

Ruxsatlar

Birinchi ishga tushirishda macOS "Ekran va tizim ovozini yozib olish", "Mikrofon" va "Kamera" ruxsatlarini so'raydi. Ekran yozish ruxsatini berganingizdan so'ng ilovani qayta ishga tushiring (Tizim sozlamalari → Maxfiylik va xavfsizlik).

Tahrirlash

  • S — playhead joyida bo'lish, I / O — boshini / oxirini olib tashlash, ⌫ — tanlangan bo'lakni o'chirish, ⌘Z — qaytarish.
  • Matn: "Matn" bo'limida "Qo'shish"; matnni video ustida sudrab joylashtiring.
  • Musiqa: "Musiqa" bo'limida faylni tanlang (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF).
  • Eksport: ⌘E — YouTube uchun tayyor MP4 (H.264, AAC).

Support (English)

RYS records your Mac screen (with system audio, microphone and webcam) and lets you trim, add text and music, then export an MP4 ready for YouTube. Shortcut ⌃⌥R starts/stops recording. Free version: recordings up to 5 minutes, export up to 1080p with a small "RYS" badge; RYS Pro removes all limits. Questions: anvarjon9101@gmail.com
