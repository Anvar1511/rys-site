RYS – Record Your Screen: Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-09-21

Qisqacha

RYS hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga yubormaydi. Ilova internetga ma'lumot uzatmaydi. Barcha yozuvlar, eksport qilingan videolar va loyiha fayllari faqat sizning kompyuteringizda saqlanadi.

Ruxsatlar

  • Ekranni yozib olish — ekran va tizim ovozini video faylga yozish uchun. Tasvir faqat siz tanlagan faylga saqlanadi.
  • Mikrofon — ovozingizni yozuvga qo'shish uchun (faqat siz yoqsangiz).
  • Kamera — veb-kamera tasvirini yozuvga qo'shish uchun (faqat siz yoqsangiz).
  • Movies (Filmlar) papkasi — yozuvlarni ~/Movies/RYS ga saqlash uchun.

Bu ruxsatlarni istalgan vaqt Tizim sozlamalari → Maxfiylik va xavfsizlik bo'limida bekor qilishingiz mumkin.

Xaridlar

RYS Pro xaridlari Apple App Store orqali amalga oshiriladi. Ilova to'lov ma'lumotlaringizni ko'rmaydi va saqlamaydi. Obunalar Apple ID sozlamalarida boshqariladi.

Analitika va reklama

Ilovada analitika, kuzatuv (tracking) va reklama yo'q.

RYS – Record Your Screen: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Summary

RYS does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app does not send any data over the internet. All recordings, exported videos and project files stay on your computer.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording — to save the screen and system audio to a video file you choose.
  • Microphone — to add your voice to a recording (only when enabled by you).
  • Camera — to add your webcam to a recording (only when enabled by you).
  • Movies folder — to save recordings to ~/Movies/RYS.

Purchases

RYS Pro is sold through the Apple App Store. The app never sees or stores your payment details. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, tracking or ads.
